# NYC TLC Trip Record Data - Schema Reference

Auto-generated by inspecting the parquet files in this directory.

## Overview

NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission (TLC) trip records, published monthly as Apache
Parquet files (one file per month). Four service classes are present:

| Service | Directory | Files | Total rows | Coverage |
|---------|-----------|-------|------------|----------|
| Yellow taxi | `yellow/` | 139 | 827,919,879 | 2015-01 .. 2026-07 |
| Green taxi | `green/` | 139 | 68,535,481 | 2015-01 .. 2026-07 |
| For-Hire Vehicle (FHV) | `fhv/` | 138 | 807,437,791 | 2015-01 .. 2026-06 |
| High-Volume FHV (HVFHV) | `fhvhv/` | 90 | 1,628,196,253 | 2019-02 .. 2026-07 |

**File naming:** `<service>_tripdata_<YYYY>-<MM>.parquet`

**Common conventions**

- One row = one completed trip.
- Datetimes are timezone-naive `timestamp[us]` (local New York wall time).
- `PULocationID` / `DOLocationID` are TLC taxi-zone IDs (1..265); join to the
  taxi zone lookup table for borough/zone names. `0` and `264`/`265` have special
  meanings (unknown / NA).
- Physical types drift across months (see "Schema evolution" below). Historically
  the same logical field may be `int64`, `double`, `int32`, `string`, or
  `large_string` depending on the month.
- Column names are case-sensitive as written and change spelling across years
  (e.g. `airport_fee` vs `Airport_fee`).

## Data quality observations

- **Out-of-range timestamps.** Row-group statistics show yellow pickup times
  ranging from `2001-01-01` to `2098-09-11`, and green from `2008-10-21` to
  `2081-06-24` - clear outliers that require filtering before time-series use.
  FHV/HVFHV pickup ranges are clean within their coverage windows.
- **Null clusters.** In recent yellow files, a block of roughly 1M rows per
  month has nulls simultaneously in `RatecodeID`, `payment_type`, and
  `store_and_fwd_flag` (and `payment_type` value `0`), likely alternate
  providers / unknown-trip records.
- **Mixed null-vs-zero encoding.** Fees such as `cbd_congestion_fee`,
  `Airport_fee`, `congestion_surcharge`, and `ehail_fee` may be `0`, null, or
  absent depending on era - normalize before aggregating.
- **Type instability.** The same column can be `int` in one month and `double`
  in another; cast explicitly when concatenating months.
